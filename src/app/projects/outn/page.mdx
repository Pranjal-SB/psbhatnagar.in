# outn

<p className="cs-meta">machine learning · image recognition · python</p>

Point it at a picture of a Pokémon and it tells you which one. An image
classifier trained on Pokémon sprites and screenshots, wrapped in the smallest
interface that gets an answer out.

## the problem

Pokédex apps make you already know the name. Reverse image search gives you
fan-art and merch listings. The gap is narrow and specific: you have a picture,
you want a name, nothing else.

## approach

One model, one job. No app shell, no accounts, no gallery. The scope is the
feature: the write-up is short because the project is.

## what it taught me

Data beats architecture at this scale. Most of the accuracy came from cleaning
and balancing the training set, not from a bigger network.
